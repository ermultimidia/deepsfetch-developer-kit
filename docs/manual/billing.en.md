# Plans, subscription and billing

Check the catalog and manage your subscription in the dashboard.

## Choose a plan

Pricing reads the system’s active catalog. Compare currency, billing interval and credits. Annual amounts represent the full year. Open Billing in your workspace and follow the available checkout; review its summary before confirming. Operation availability also depends on configured providers, not only the plan.

- [/pricing](https://deepsfetch.com/pricing)
- [/app/billing](https://deepsfetch.com/app/billing)


## Change or cancel

Use the actions available in Billing and the billing portal to manage your subscription. Check the updated status and displayed dates after a change or cancellation. Do not assume refunds, proration or immediate cancellation unless confirmed by the flow. Subscription cancellation and job cancellation differ; also review scheduled automations and collections.


## Usage and discrepancies

Compare usage and reservations in Cost controls with subscription status in Billing. A monthly credit cap is not a BRL/USD charge. If checkout finishes but the dashboard has not updated, query again before starting another purchase. Record workspace, time and transaction identifier for support, without card details. No additional public per-plan feature matrix is specified beyond the displayed catalog; check resource limits in its guide.


## Step by step: subscribe and verify

Current catalog is the price reference; inspect the displayed offer before subscribing.

1. Open Pricing, compare currency, interval and credits. Annual amounts are yearly totals.
2. In your workspace open billing through My account and follow checkout. Inspect its summary and confirm only the intended offer.
3. After return refresh the dashboard and confirm subscription, dates and available credits; do not buy twice because an update is delayed.
4. For change/cancellation use the available portal and inspect new state/dates. Pause automations you do not intend to retain.
5. For discrepancies record workspace, time and transaction ID; do not send card data or secrets to support.
